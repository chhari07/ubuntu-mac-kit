## Modifications

- `WhiteSur-dark/places` and `WhiteSur-dark/mimes`: folder and file-type icons were replaced with icons generated by
  `tools/mac-file-icons-gen.py` from this repository. All other icons are unchanged.
- Cached files (`icon-theme.cache`) are removed from the bundle and rebuilt during installation.
